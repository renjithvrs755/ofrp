# TEST-0002 — Reported Speaker Output Pair Resistance Check

> **PASSIVE TEST ONLY — NOT RUN:** Disconnect all power, sources, speakers, and
> vehicle wiring. Confirm zero unexpected voltage before resistance mode. The
> reported pairs are `V1` candidates, not verified output assignments.

> **SAFETY GATE — NO POWER:** Do not apply power to the amplifier until:
> - connector orientation is verified,
> - battery positive is verified,
> - ground is verified,
> - no output pin is shorted, and
> - supply is fused and current-limited.
